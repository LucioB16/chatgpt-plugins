Iterloom

Turn chats into AI upgrades.

Iterloom examines the ChatGPT conversation context actually available for your request and can also work with conversation exports you explicitly provide. It identifies repeated friction, patterns, and opportunities to improve your AI setup.

What you can do

  • Spot recurring problems: group friction points across accessible conversations.
  • Prioritize improvements: rank actionable changes to plugins, integrations, and workflows using observed evidence.
  • Understand coverage: see which conversations or export files could actually be reviewed, and which conclusions remain uncertain.

How it works

Ask for a retrospective and choose the context or export data to analyze. Iterloom produces recommendations; it does not independently retrieve your entire ChatGPT history or automatically reconfigure external tools.

Iterloom is a skills-only plugin. Your selected context and any provided export are processed in the host AI environment; the plugin has no developer-operated conversation-collection endpoint.
